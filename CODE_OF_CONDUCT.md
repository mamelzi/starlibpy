# Starlibpy Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Starlibpy community is committed to a respectful, inclusive, professional,
and scientifically constructive environment for everyone, regardless of
background, identity, experience, discipline, institution, or level of
technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and patiently;
- critique ideas, code, and methods rather than people;
- distinguish evidence, interpretation, and opinion;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently;
- respect confidentiality, privacy, authorship, and attribution;
- welcome beginners and explain specialized terminology;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- follow applicable research, clinical, ethical, and legal standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- deliberate intimidation or public humiliation;
- publishing private information without permission;
- plagiarism or misrepresentation of authorship;
- knowingly concealing a statistical defect that may alter research results;
- retaliating against a person who reports a concern;
- sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
project events, and other spaces where a person represents Starlibpy.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact
method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue
containing sensitive personal information.

The maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, close discussions,
or take other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as reasonably possible, with attention to fairness and safety.
